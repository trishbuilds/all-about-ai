**How to grow the list to 50:** open the top AI post in your feed each day and follow 1 or 2 people who leave thoughtful comments. Search "AI" in posts, filter to Past week, and follow authors with unusually high engagement for their size. Mute anyone who mostly posts engagement bait.

## Daily sweep (10 min)

1. Switch the feed to **Most recent**.
2. Check **LinkedIn News** (top stories on the right of the feed) for any AI or tech story.
3. Save 3 to 5 posts with unusually high comments for that author, or a strong opinion or real-world example.
4. Paste each link into the Notion **Drop** page with one line on why it caught your eye.
